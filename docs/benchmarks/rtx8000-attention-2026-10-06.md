# RTX 8000 long-context attention, direct measurements

The pinned Prism CUDA attention dispatcher uses an eight-head MMA group for the Bonsai 2 27B model's 6:1 GQA, 256-dimensional heads. Two group columns are masked. The committed SM75 overlay selects the existing two-head MMA specialization for this geometry; all other head geometries and devices retain the pinned selection. It limits this selection to query batches above eight, leaving decode batches 1/2/4/8 alone. The reduced padded work is a plausible mechanism, not an isolated hardware-counter attribution: the two-head mapping can reread K/V across three groups.

The RTX 8000 stock endpoint remained unchanged. All measurements used direct CUDA runtimes with Q8/Q8 KV, no calibration, and no containers. Robot `running_runs.total` was zero before each build or GPU load. A direct `test-backend-ops` performance case used 24 query heads, four KV heads, head dimension 256 and a 2,048-query prefill chunk. The same staged test binary was run with the stock and candidate CUDA libraries. These are one matched benchmark per case, with the harness's internal graph replays:

| KV depth | Stock Q8 attention | Two-head Q8 attention | Time reduction |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 65,536 | 137.151 ms | 101.496 ms | 26.0% |
| 225,280 | 473.630 ms | 346.612 ms | 26.8% |

At 65,536 KV, F16/F16 took 135.205 ms stock and 100.011 ms with the candidate. The near-tie between stock Q8 and F16 at this depth shows that Q8-to-F16 conversion is not the dominant long-context cost. The candidate passed both Q8 and F16 CPU-reference attention cases at 6:1 GQA, 512 KV tokens and 16 query tokens (2/2). The 225K performance case was not CPU-reference checked.

The runtime built from committed engine revision `644da4a` passed the same two CPU-reference cases and reproduced the 225,280-token Q8 result at **345.322 ms** (27.1% below stock). Its compiled CUDA library SHA-256 is `ca9b90ff097d7e9b1a672788f69e86c12da6cb7ad44b8ef69e2efbb2fe6d3ff4`.

Two `llama-bench` direct model runs used 225,000 prompt tokens and ten generated tokens, Q8/Q8 KV, batch 4,096, microbatch 2,048, flash attention and CUDA operation tracing. The isolated attention-only candidate used the stock matrix path and completed in 602.369 seconds. The committed engine forced its custom CUTLASS prefill and DP4A decode paths without calibration and completed in **583.927 seconds**. The saved stock Prism trace used 226,000 prompt tokens and completed in 723.045 seconds, so whole-run wall times are **not** an exact pair. The first 109 full 2,048-token prefill chunks have the same depth and graph shapes, and are compared below:

| First 109 full chunks | Stock Prism | Attention only | Combined custom | Combined reduction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| All traced operations | 696.759 s | 583.018 s | 564.733 s | 18.9% |
| `FLASH_ATTN_EXT` | 432.931 s | 319.551 s | 320.267 s | 26.0% |
| Operations containing `MUL_MAT` | 204.440 s | 204.341 s | 184.816 s | 9.6% |
| Other operations | 59.387 s | 59.125 s | 59.650 s | −0.4% |

Traced Q8 decode time was 0.817 s for ten tokens on stock and attention-only, and 0.789 s on the combined engine. The Q8 decode attention path remained vector attention. The combined run's sampled dispatch log has 298 custom CUTLASS prefill and 259 custom DP4A decode selections, all `forced_diagnostic`, with no recorded fallback; these records are selection evidence, not total operation counts. The matched direct prefill trace beats stock by **18.9%**, above the 10% target. `llama-bench` did not compare generated output content, and this is not yet an endpoint measurement.

The RTX 8000 had 18,552 MiB used before the combined direct model load, 35,184 MiB used with the model loaded and early prefill underway, and 18,552 MiB after exit. The **16,632 MiB increase is total device use**, not an isolated weight allocation. The `.30` build compiled successfully but could not copy a second bundle under its user disk quota.
